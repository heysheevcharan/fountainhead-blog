This blog is an attempt to meet like-minded people, and I hope it creates enough digital gravity to attract them.

Three core values of my life:

**1/ Entrepreneurial freedom**

I have always been entrepreneurial. As a kid, I was collecting and selling computer game cheat codes. Later on, I sold odd things like event tickets, banners, and decorative paints.

**2/ Thoughtful products and design**

At the age of 12, my dad gifted me an [iPod nano gen 3](https://apple.fandom.com/wiki/IPod_nano_(3rd_generation)). I loved it so much that I used to carry that thing around all the time.

**3/ Understanding human nature**

In high school, my dad brought me [How to Talk to Anyone](https://www.amazon.in/How-Talk-Anyone-Success-Relationships/dp/0007272618). I immediately applied the methods on my friends. I still do that now, whenever I learn something new about human behavior.
