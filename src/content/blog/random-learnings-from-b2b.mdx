1. Avoid building tools to replace low-income, low-frequency jobs.

2. Solve problems that drive revenue and become associated with success.

3. Look at a globally large space if you want to build a brand; focus on a small niche if you want to build a lifestyle business.

4. ROI is the only metric when selling to B2B—either lower the investment or increase the return.

5. Use AI to automate higher-order intelligent jobs. It’s hard, but it’s worth it.

6. When you pick a hypothesis, define a timeframe to validate it.

7. If you’re solving for efficiency optimization:  
• A lot of people have the inefficiency, big cost - good to go.  
• A lot of people have the inefficiency, small cost - maybe.  
• A few people have the inefficiency, small cost - avoid.  
• A few people have the inefficiency, big cost - depends on the money.
