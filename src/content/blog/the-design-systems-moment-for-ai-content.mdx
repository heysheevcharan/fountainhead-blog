Internet users will drown in AI-generated content. The brands who survive won't be the ones who generate the most. They'll be the ones who generate the most narratively coherent content.

If you're a brand leader today, this is your design systems moment.

As generation of single AI video and image becomes easy and simple - companies investing in narrative-driven, brand-compliant content systems will capture user attention and scale effectively.

Think of it this way, in the last generation, people invested heavily in design systems and brand guidelines. But those guardrails alone would not help them build content in an AI world. This AI systems must work with non-determinism and still deliver content that resonates with the audience emotionally and visually.

Let's take an example: the infamous cat videos on YouTube and Instagram - they are cringe, but you watch till the end without realising it. That's the narrative pulling you. Not the production quality. Now think about the last piece of content your brand put out. What was pulling the audience?

Hypothetically, what if these cat videos are generated by a cat food brand? Next they have a system that can generate such content at scale with near perfect narrative coherence and brand compliance? They own the attention of the category. Your category has a version of this waiting to happen.

This is not hypothetical for long. Such systems will be built by every company - be it in Banking, FMCG, Automotive, and more.

Mega-brands of tomorrow will be built on AI native systems that can generate emotionally compelling, and visually coherent content. The core story of the brand will be crafted by humans, but the content will be crafted by AI.

Are you building this system?
