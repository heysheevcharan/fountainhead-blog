## Why I'm writing

I've been meaning to start writing publicly for a while. The goal is simple:
document what I'm learning, think through ideas in public, and occasionally
share something useful.

Posts here will mostly cover software engineering — things I'm building,
problems I've run into, and concepts I find interesting. No fixed schedule,
no theme.
